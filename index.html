Our Story

Where Passion Meets
Perfection

Founded in 2001 by Chef Antoine Dubois, La Maison Dorée has been a beacon of culinary excellence in the heart of the city. Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that dining is not merely sustenance, but an art form that engages all the senses.

Every ingredient is sourced from local farms and artisan producers, ensuring the freshest flavors while supporting our community. Our kitchen is a stage where tradition meets innovation, creating dishes that honor classical French techniques while embracing modern creativity.

Chef Antoine Dubois

Executive Chef & Founder
